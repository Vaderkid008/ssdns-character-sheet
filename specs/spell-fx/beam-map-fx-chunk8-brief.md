## Chunk 8 — 10 slots (L2–3 combat mid)

### 1. Spike Growth — cholla patch (**sticky zone**)
- **Vibe:** The ground grows a bed of nails.
- **Motion:** Spikes/cholla erupt across the area; idle hazard.
- **Impact:** Piercing tick flares on move-through. Sticky zone + DM Clear. Distinct from Entangle (grab) and Spike vs briar pin.

### 2. Mirror Image — decoy selves
- **Vibe:** Three of you, only one bleeds.
- **Motion:** On cast, 1–3 translucent duplicate silhouettes orbit/offset the caster.
- **Impact:** When an image is popped: puff dissolve. Idle duplicates stay low-alpha. Not create-tokens (follow caster).

### 3. Blur — heat shimmer (self)
- **Vibe:** Heat haze over the gunfighter.
- **Motion:** Persistent shimmer shell on the caster while concentrating.
- **Impact:** Attack-miss can show a brief “swish through haze.” Keep token readable.

### 4. Silence — hush dome (**sticky zone**)
- **Vibe:** Sound dies in a bubble.
- **Motion:** Clear dome/sphere on aim; subtle rim shimmer (no loud VFX).
- **Impact:** Mute cue icon on creatures inside optional. Sticky + DM Clear.

### 5. Counterspell — interrupt flash
- **Vibe:** You snuff their cast mid-draw.
- **Motion:** Reaction: sharp force/arcane flash from counterspeller toward the caster being interrupted (or on that caster).
- **Impact:** Spell fizzle burst on the target caster. Very short (~150 ms).

### 6. Fear — run-you-off wave
- **Vibe:** Terror rolls out of your stare.
- **Motion:** Cone/wave of fear wisps from the caster.
- **Impact:** Fear **token wrap** (flee cue) on failed saves. Shared psychic/fear family with Phantasmal Killer / Dissonant, different shape (cone).

### 7. Haste — stopwatch speed (**token wrap**)
- **Vibe:** Everything around them slows.
- **Motion:** Speed-line / afterimage trails on the hasted creature.
- **Impact:** Haste **token wrap** (blur trail) while up. Optional BA-action flash when they spend the extra action.

### 8. Slow — molasses (**token wrap**)
- **Vibe:** Time thickens around them.
- **Motion:** Heavy amber drip / drag trails on affected creatures.
- **Impact:** Slow **token wrap** opposite Haste. Shared slot family OK as two skins on one review tile strip.

### 9. Hypnotic Pattern — swinging watch
- **Vibe:** A mesmer pattern hangs in the air.
- **Motion:** Soft hypnotic swirl / watch-glint pattern fills a cube; idle loop preferred (like Chunk 4 idle overlays).
- **Impact:** Charmed/incap **token wrap** (glazed eyes / swirl mark) on fails. Sticky pattern zone until broken / DM Clear.

### 10. Fly — aeronaut trails
- **Vibe:** Boot soles leave the dirt.
- **Motion:** Lift puff on cast; ongoing altitude cue (shadow shrink / dust under feet) while flying.
- **Impact:** Optional swoop trail on dash. Not a winged angel — grit levitation / wind coat.
