## Chunk 9 — 5 slots (L3–4 set-piece)

### 1. Stinking Cloud — rotten eggs (**sticky zone**)
- **Vibe:** A bank of gut-roiling fog.
- **Motion:** Olive-brown stinking fog fills the area; idle drift.
- **Impact:** Poison/retch tick flares; poisoned **token wrap** optional. Sticky + DM Clear. Distinct from Cloudkill (death fog) and Incendiary (fire fog).

### 2. Wind Wall — dust wall (**sticky barrier**)
- **Vibe:** A standing gale you can lean a board on.
- **Motion:** Vertical wind ribbon along the aimed line (aim-then-cast); dust and grit stream.
- **Impact:** Projectiles wink out / deflect sparks on the wall. Sticky barrier + DM Clear. Distinct from Wall of Fire / Ice / Thorns / Blade.

### 3. Banishment — boot to elsewhere
- **Vibe:** You kick them into the Outer Dark for a spell.
- **Motion:** Teal/void portal wink swallows the target; empty square shimmer while banished.
- **Impact:** On return: reverse wink into the square. Shared family with Banishing Smite (smite is on-hit flash; this is full swallow).

### 4. Black Tentacles — nightmare noose zone (**sticky zone** + **token wrap**)
- **Vibe:** Hadar’s cousins boil up from the dirt.
- **Motion:** Dark tentacles fill the area (idle writhe — loop candidate).
- **Impact:** Grapple/restrain → tentacle **token wrap**. Sticky zone + DM Clear. Darker/thicker than Arms of Hadar’s short lash; lasting like Hunger but tentacles not void fog.

### 5. Polymorph — beast swap flash
- **Vibe:** Man becomes coyote (or worse) in a puff.
- **Motion:** Transform bloom on the target; silhouette morphs to beast token/shape.
- **Impact:** Brief morph flash; new form is the map token (Coder swap). FX is the bloom + leftover clothes/dust puff, not a permanent second FX.
