## Chunk 7 — 10 slots (L1–2 control / buff / staple zones)

### 1. Entangle — brush snare (**sticky zone**)
- **Vibe:** The scrub grabs ankles.
- **Motion:** On cast, briars/weeds erupt across the aim area (idle sway).
- **Impact:** Creatures in the zone get vine-grab flares; restrained → **token wrap** vines. Sticky until duration / DM Clear. Same briar family as Thorn Whip / Ensnaring Strike.

### 2. Faerie Fire — foxfire outline (**token wrap**)
- **Vibe:** Everyone lit up like a claim stake.
- **Motion:** Short cast sparkle → each failed-save target gets a glowing outline.
- **Impact:** Persistent foxfire **token wrap** (advantage cue) while the spell lasts. Low-alpha neon grit, not cartoon neon.

### 3. Sleep — chloroform dust
- **Vibe:** A soft knock-out cloud.
- **Motion:** A gentle dust/mist bloom on the aim point; motes settle on the lowest-HP targets in range.
- **Impact:** Sleepy Z grit / closed-eye mark on dropped creatures (brief). Not a combat nuke bloom.

### 4. Bane — jinx (**token wrap**)
- **Vibe:** Bad luck sticks to them.
- **Motion:** Short jinx flick caster → each target.
- **Impact:** Small curse brand **token wrap** (d4 penalty cue) while concentrating. Pair visually opposite Bless (named-v1).

### 5. Armor of Agathys — cold iron hide (self)
- **Vibe:** Frost plate that bites back.
- **Motion:** Ice shell wraps the **caster** on cast (held/self FX).
- **Impact:** When a melee attacker hits the caster: cold snap retaliatory bloom on the attacker (Fire Shield–adjacent, cold only). Idle shell stays readable.

### 6. Color Spray — limelight fan
- **Vibe:** A pocket flashbang of colored light.
- **Motion:** Short cone/fan of grit color flares from the hand (~200 ms).
- **Impact:** Blind/stun flash on caught creatures. Keep saloon grit, not rainbow party.

### 7. Darkness — black hat (**sticky zone**; Lighting v2 still Phase 4)
- **Vibe:** A hatful of night drops on the square.
- **Motion:** Opaque black sphere/zone on aim (or on a point object). Idle void.
- **Impact:** Vision block cue; **sticky zone** + DM Clear. True lighting occlusion stays Phase 4 — this is the zone silhouette + fog partnership.

### 8. Hold Person — lasso (**token wrap**)
- **Vibe:** An invisible rope cinches them still.
- **Motion:** Lasso / binding flash caster → target.
- **Impact:** Paralyzed **token wrap** (rope cinch / frozen pose cue) while held. Save shake = brief rope strain.

### 9. Misty Step — shortcut puff
- **Vibe:** You vanish in dust and reappear a jump away.
- **Motion:** Smoke/dust pop at origin → matching pop at destination (BA teleport).
- **Impact:** Brief after-image at both ends. No travel beam.

### 10. Web — flypaper (**sticky zone**)
- **Vibe:** A spider-silk net fills the air.
- **Motion:** Web sheets fill the aim cube/sphere; idle strands.
- **Impact:** Restrained → web **token wrap**. Sticky zone + DM Clear / fire-burn clear garnish optional.
