# Bitter Spring Relay Station: DM Run Sheet
**SSDNS showcase test, Sat Nov 14 2026.** Built from Encounter Bot's README.md, encounter.json, both layout keys and PREGENS.md. Stand-alone and spoiler-free.
**4 PCs, level 3** (pregens) · **Medium** · about **60–90 min** · two floors, 32×26 squares, squares line up between floors.

---

## 1. One-glance opening

**Pitch.** A two-storey stage relay on a dry road, mid-afternoon. Road agents grabbed the stage when it pulled in. They tied up the driver and the station agent upstairs and dragged the strongbox into the store room, and now they're prying at it. They want the party watered and gone before the lid gives. The party can talk, snoop or fight. When it comes to a fight, it goes **up**: balcony, hayloft, stairwell. Odds are somebody ends up in the horse trough.

**Hook (pick one):**
- **Water:** Bitter Spring is the only water on this road for a day.
- **Catch the stage:** they have tickets, or they want a ride on the next leg.
- **A letter to post:** this relay is where the mail gets handed to the stage.

**Read-aloud:**
> A two-storey clapboard station sits back from the road, with a balcony across the front and a barn to the east. A stagecoach stands in the road with no horses in the traces. A man in a long coat leans on the balcony rail with a rifle in the crook of his arm. "Station's closed, friends. Trough's yours. Then keep moving." On the porch below him, a big man taps a pick handle against his boot. Somewhere inside, iron scrapes on iron.

**Setup:** party at A2–B4 (stage road, west). The optional back-lot group (C25–D26) is for a split party or late arrivals. Start the **Crack** clock at 0 and the **Suspicion** clock at 0. **Getaway** isn't running yet.

## 2. Phase-by-phase flow

### Phase 1: Talk
- **Goal:** let the party read the scene. The crew wants them gone quickly and quietly.
- **Staging:** the Ramrod talks from the gallery (upstairs L12). Pick-Handle keeps everyone off the porch. Let them water up at the trough (I10–J11), right under the busted rail.
- **Clocks running:** Suspicion and Crack.
- **Key checks (talk moves):**

| Move | DC | Result |
|:--|:-:|:--|
| Persuasion: "We'll water up and go" | **14** | They're let go. If they leave, use *Party leaves early* (Endings). |
| Deception: "We're from the stage line, the guards are a mile back" | **15** | Crack +1. The Ramrod sends the Barkeep upstairs to "check on the agent," which opens a gap. **Fail by 5+ = fight.** |
| Deception: "We're here for a cut" | **13** | One free action before the fight, or a walk into the taproom. **Fail by 5+ = fight.** |
| Intimidation | **15** | Pick-Handle steps aside. In the fight, the Barkeep and the Stall Hand each surrender the first time they're hit. |
| Stealth round the back (kitchen door T23) | **12** | Against passive Perception: crew **10**, Ramrod **15**. On a success they're inside unseen. |
| Sleight of Hand: lift the store-room key off the Barkeep | **14** | Opens the store-room door with no roll and no noise. |

- **Talk-only win:** the guards Deception (DC 15), **then** a second Deception or Intimidation (DC 13), **before Crack hits 6**. The crew grabs nothing, leaves the captives and rides out east. The party frees the captives and opens the box at leisure. **Full XP.**
- **Moves on to Tension when:** Suspicion hits **3**, or the party starts snooping (store-room door, barn, back door).

### Phase 2: Tension
- **Goal:** the smiles are gone. Every move now is a push. Let the players feel the box giving.
- **What's happening:** at Suspicion **3** the Ramrod says "Water your horses and ride on," and the crew moves to their spots. Crack keeps ticking. Crack 3 is the lock groaning, which anyone in the taproom hears with no check.
- **Key checks:** the clue table (section 4), the talk moves above, Pick lock or Force on the store-room door (Pick **12** / Force **15**, loud), and the tripwire on the porch steps (spot DC **13**).
- **Moves on to the Fight when:** Suspicion is at **4** and someone pushes again; a Deception fails by **5+**; someone Forces a door; someone climbs toward the balcony; or the party strikes first.

### Phase 3: Fight
- **Goal:** survive, free the captives, keep the box.
- **Initiative:** the Ramrod and Pick-Handle each roll their own. The Cracker and the Barkeep share one Outlaw roll. The Loft Rifle and the Stall Hand share one Rustler roll. If DMCC won't group them, roll each one separately; it still works.
- **Surprise:** **none** if the fight comes out of talk. If the party sneaks in the back and strikes first, normal 5e surprise applies to anyone who hadn't noticed them.
- **Hidden:** the Loft Rifle and the Stall Hand start hidden (**fixed Stealth 12**). The first shot from an unseen rifle has **advantage**. The Cracker is behind a locked door.
- **Round 1 crew script:**
  - **Ramrod (E1):** two Henrietta shots from the gallery (half cover). He holds the gallery until "Ride!"
  - **Pick-Handle (E2):** Shoves the first PC up the porch steps back into the tripwire (K12–L12), or knocks them prone, then clubs. Pack Tactics.
  - **Cracker (E3):** keeps prying (Crack +1). Once the door opens, he shoots, then Takes Cover behind the crates. "Come in and I light the keg!" (bluff unless the Ramrod is down).
  - **Barkeep (E4):** shoots over the bar. **Round 2:** goes up the inside stairs (O23 to O18) to back the Ramrod.
  - **Loft Rifle (E5):** kicks open the hay door (AD13) and fires into the yard.
  - **Stall Hand (E6):** shoots from the barn doors. Once Getaway starts, he leads horses to the doors.
  - **Lookout (R1, optional):** rides in at AF3 at the **top of round 3** and joins the Outlaw turn. Only use him if it's going too easy.
- **Morale:** when **two crew are down**, or the **Ramrod drops below half HP (under 8)**, he yells **"Ride!"** and Getaway starts. If the Ramrod drops, everyone left makes a **DC 10 Wis** save. On a failure they surrender or run for the barn.
- **Misfire / jam:** Ball n Cap on a **1–4**, Henrietta on a **1–2**.
- **Moves on to Loot when:** the crew is down, has surrendered or has run; or Getaway hits **4**.

### Phase 4: Loot
- **Goal:** open the boxes, free the captives, hand out XP.
- **Flow:** Interact > **Pick lock** / **Force** > loot window > **Take all**. Every box is hand-placed, so nothing gets rolled.
- **Key checks:** the container DCs (section 7), and the hidden saddle chest (Perception **13**).
- **Three ways into the strongbox:** pick it (**15**), force it (**25**), or carry the **Strongbox Key** down from the agent's office chest. That last one is the up-and-down-the-stairs test.
- **Crew pockets:** roll the usual bestiary drop tables. Any gun taken off the crew is **Dirty**. The party gets **one clean gun** per encounter: the Ramrod's Henrietta or one Ball n Cap.

**Endings**

| Outcome | What happens |
|:--|:--|
| **Clean win** (captives freed, box not carried off) | The agent hands over the Strongbox Key if they haven't found it, and lets them take the Field Medic Kit and the Letter as a reward. The driver offers a free seat on the next leg. |
| **Messy win** (crew beaten, Crack hit 6) | Get the 400 ES back off the Cracker, or catch him in the Getaway chase. |
| **They got away** (Getaway 4) | The surviving crew are gone with whatever they carried. The captives are still upstairs, behind a locked door. |
| **Party leaves early** | Down the road they hear the stage was robbed at Bitter Spring. The driver is asking for them. |
| **Party loses** (everyone down) | The crew takes their cash and one gun each, then locks them in the passenger room. The agent works them loose at dusk. |

Anyone still on foot is left behind. |

## 5. NPC quick cards

Stat blocks come from the live bestiary (`bestiary-full.json`). AC, HP, CR and XP below are from the source. Attack and damage lines are in the bestiary and are **not set in source**.

**E1 · The Ramrod** (`rival-adventurer`) · CR 1/2 · **AC 13 · HP 16** · Init: own roll (Dex +2) · Upstairs, L12 (gallery)
- **Role:** the talker and the boss. Carries the **passenger-room key**.
- **Voice:** easy, friendly, never in a hurry. He smiles with his mouth only and leans on the rail like he owns it. Gets quieter the angrier he gets.
- "Station's closed, friends. Trough's yours. Then keep moving."
- "Agent's sick. Water your horses and ride on."
- In a fight he fires two Henrietta shots from behind the rail. When he calls **"Ride!"** he heads down the outside stairs (X13) for the barn.

**E2 · Pick-Handle** (`company-rider`, shown as "Pick-Handle") · CR 1/2 · **AC 11 · HP 32** · Init: own roll (Dex +0) · Ground, M13 (porch)
- **Role:** porch muscle. Pack Tactics. Never leaves the porch until "Ride!"
- **Voice:** slow, one word where five would do. Taps the pick handle on his boot before he answers.
- "Porch is closed too."
- "Mind the step."

**E3 · The Cracker** (`outlaw`) · CR 1/8 · **AC 12 · HP 11** · Init: shares the Outlaw roll · Ground, R16 (locked store room, **hidden**)
- **Role:** prying at the strongbox. Each turn alone in there is Crack +1.
- **Voice:** sweaty, breathless and twitchy, talking to the box more than to people.
- "Come in and I light the keg!" (bluff, unless the Ramrod is down)
- "Almost... almost, you son of a..."

**E4 · Barkeep** (`outlaw`) · CR 1/8 · **AC 12 · HP 11** · Init: shares the Outlaw roll · Ground, J22 (behind the bar)
- **Role:** a crewman playing barkeep. Carries the **store-room key**. Surrenders the first time he's hit if the party won with Intimidation.
- **Voice:** too helpful. Polishes the same glass the whole time and doesn't know where anything is kept.
- "Water's free. Whiskey ain't. Agent's orders."
- "Store room? Just flour and such. Rats, mostly."

**E5 · Loft Rifle** (`rustler`) · CR 1/8 · **AC 12 · HP 11** · Init: shares the Rustler roll · Upstairs, AD13 (behind the hay door, **hidden**, Stealth 12)
- **Role:** overwatch on the yard. Round 1 he kicks the hay door open and fires down.
- **Voice:** silent until he shoots. If he's cornered, he's a kid who wants his mother.
- "I see three of 'em, boss. Four, counting the ugly one."

**E6 · Stall Hand** (`rustler`) · CR 1/8 · **AC 12 · HP 11** · Init: shares the Rustler roll · Ground, Z18 (barn stalls, **hidden**, Stealth 12)
- **Role:** horse holder for the getaway. Shoots from the barn doors and brings the horses up once Getaway starts. Surrenders the first time he's hit if the party won with Intimidation.
- **Voice:** mutters to the horses, not the people.
- "Easy, girl. Easy. Nobody's shootin' you."

**R1 · Lookout** (`outlaw`, optional) · CR 1/8 · **AC 12 · HP 11** · Ground, AF3, top of round 3
- **Role:** rides back in at the sound of gunfire. Only use him if it's going too easy.
- "Heard the shootin' from a mile out. You boys done yet?"

**B1 · Stage driver** (`common-townsfolk`) · **AC 10 · HP 4** · Upstairs, S21, tied up, hidden
- **Voice:** sore, salty and grateful in that order.
- "Took you long enough. Who's minding my stage?"
- If freed alive, he offers a free seat on the next leg.

**B2 · Station agent** (`common-townsfolk`) · **AC 10 · HP 4** · Upstairs, U22, tied up, hidden
- **Knows:** the **Strongbox Key** is in his office chest (C2, U14).
- **Voice:** precise and fussy, the company's man even with a gag in. Worried about the paperwork.
- "The key's in my chest, in the office. Mind you sign for it."

## 9. If things go sideways

- **TPK risk:**
  - Leave the Lookout home.
  - Have the Cracker keep prying instead of shooting. Every turn he spends on the box is a turn he isn't shooting anyone.
  - Call "Ride!" as soon as it triggers. The crew would rather leave than finish anybody.
  - If everyone goes down, use *Party loses*: they get locked in the passenger room, and the agent works them loose at dusk. Nobody dies in a demo.
- **Party skips the talk and starts shooting:**
  - Roll initiative. There's no surprise unless they came in unseen.
  - Crack still ticks on the Cracker's turns, so the box becomes the timer.
  - Point them at the clues mid-fight: the thumping upstairs (DC 10), the horses in harness (DC 12).
- **A captive is about to die (4 HP each):**
  - Nothing in the source targets the captives. Keep them out of the line of fire unless a PC puts them in it.
  - If one drops, Sister Ruth has Spare the Dying, plus Healing Word and Cure Wounds. June and Dell have Healing Word. C3 has the Healing Libation and the Cure Wounds Letter, in the same room as the captives.
- **Clue trail stalls:**
  - Crack 3 is free: anyone in the taproom hears the lock groan, no check.
  - The rifle barrel is caught on passive Perception 12+.
  - If they still don't move, Crack hits 6 and Getaway forces the issue.
- **Can't get into the strongbox:**
  - Send them upstairs for the Strongbox Key (C2), or have the agent mention it once he's freed.
  - Retries are the DM's call.
- **Running long:**
  - Tick Crack on the 10-minute mark without mercy.
  - Push Suspicion to 4 and start the fight.
  - Have the Ramrod call "Ride!" at the first trigger, and let Getaway end it.
- **Party splits up across floors:**
  - Use the back-lot group (C25–D26) for the flankers.
  - Move tokens across links by hand, at 20 ft each.
- **Party walks away:** use *Party leaves early*: news of the robbery, and the driver asking for them.
- **It's a cakewalk:** bring in the Lookout at round 3. Have the Cracker light the keg once the Ramrod is down.

## 10. Changes / flags
1. **"Ride!" triggers differ.**
   - README Morale: two crew down, **or the Ramrod below half HP**.
   - encounter.json E1 tactics: two crew down, **or Crack hits 6**.
   - **Run sheet:** follows the README for the yell. Crack 6 starts Getaway anyway (both files agree on that), so the two only differ when the Ramrod drops under half HP. "Below half HP" is written here as **under 8** (16 HP ÷ 2). That's my arithmetic, not a number from the source.
2. **New: a Tension phase.** The source's run order is "Talk, then tension, then a fight", but it gives no separate Tension section. I put one together from Suspicion 3, Crack 3 and the snooping checks. No new mechanics.
3. **Store-room light differs.**
   - README: Dark until the lantern is lit or someone brings light.
   - Ground layout key: Dark until the lantern is lit **or the door stands open**.
   - **Run sheet:** didn't settle it. DM's call.
4. **Tripwire spotting:** the README clue table gives only Perception/Investigation DC 13. The layout key and encounter.json add **passive Perception 13**. I included both.
5. **Back-door Stealth is unclear.** It's listed as DC 12, but also "against passive Perception: crew 10, Ramrod 15." **Run sheet:** copied as written. Suggested reading: DC 12 clears the crew, and anyone the Ramrod can see needs 15.
6. **The guards Deception success also ticks Crack +1** (as written). That's unusual for a success, but I kept it.
7. **Failure results not set in source:**
   - Persuasion DC 14, Intimidation DC 15 and Sleight of Hand DC 14 have no failure result beyond the clock ticks.
   - It isn't stated whether a failed Sleight of Hand counts as a "failed social check" for Crack.
8. **"About every 10 minutes"** for Crack doesn't say whether that's real time at the table or game time. **Not set in source.**
9. **Attack and damage numbers aren't given** (Henrietta, the pick-handle club, Outlaw and Rustler weapons). They live in `bestiary-full.json`, which I didn't read. **Not set in source.**
10. **Extra details only in encounter.json:**
    - The powder blast would hit the strongbox (a 25 HP stub, no loot lost, DM call).
    - Gunfire in the barn spooks a horse 10 ft.
    - A creature shoved into the trough has its next Dex check or save "normal."
    - The Ramrod heads for the outside stairs (X13) after "Ride!"
    - Pick-Handle never leaves the porch until "Ride!"
    - I kept these short.
11. **"The Letter"** in the Clean-win ending doesn't say which one: Shield (C1) or Cure Wounds (C3). **Not set in source.**
12. **Captives:** no source rule has the crew threaten or attack them, and the agent and driver have no names. **Not set in source.**
13. **Code gap (Coder) that affects the table:**
    - `loot.js` doesn't add Athletics proficiency to Force.
    - So the sheet shows Wren at **+3**, not **+5**. At +3 even a natural 20 (23) can't force the strongbox (DC 25).
    - The PREGENS Force column is the rules-correct number. The README notes the same gap.
14. **Banned word in an item id:** encounter.json gives the Healing Libation an item id that starts with the word the plain-words rule bans (`a***y-tonic-trail-draught`, at about line 2045). Its display name is fine, and only the display name appears here. Flag for Coder or the catalog.
15. **Campaign references left out:**
    - The sources mention "Dry Fork" (an earlier showcase), "Act I strongbox default" (C1 log and README Softs), "Act I style" clocks, and Ezra's Outlaw background "per BOOK1."
