# Bitter Spring Relay — how to load the showcase

The files live on the site next to the sheet:

- Pack: https://vaderkid008.github.io/ssdns-character-sheet/showcase/bitter-spring-relay/bitter-spring-relay.ssdns-pack.json
- Pregens: https://vaderkid008.github.io/ssdns-character-sheet/showcase/bitter-spring-relay/pregens/

## DM

1. Open the DM Command Center and the map.
2. Maps, then Scene builder.
3. Import pack, and choose `bitter-spring-relay.ssdns-pack.json`.
4. The library gains **Bitter Spring Relay Station**. Load into room.
5. The ground floor opens with the party spawn spots, the strongbox, and the foes that are not hidden. Upstairs is the other floor chip.
6. Walls, including the two locked doors (pick 12, Force 15), seed the first time each floor map is opened.
7. Reveal, on a hidden foe or Reveal all, shows the ones that started hidden. Pick-Handle is the company rider's name on the map.

## Players

1. On the character sheet, Open, and choose one `.ssdns` file from `pregens/`.
2. Ace and Ezra have thieves' tools and are proficient. The +2 comes from level 3. Nell has the tools and is not proficient, so a pick is Dexterity only.

## What to test

- Two floors, 32 by 26. Party spawns on A2–B4. The other group is on C25–D26.
- Three two-way links, hidden from the players: inside stairs O23 to O18, outside stairs X20 to X13, barn ladder AE14 to AE14.
- Stage Strongbox (S16) is pick 15 / Force 25 and opens with the Strongbox Key from the Agent's Chest (U14, pick 13 / Force 15).
- Passenger Trunk (Q22) is unlocked. Crew's Saddle Chest (AF20) starts hidden, pick 14 / Force 15.
- Six foes. Three start hidden. None of the names on the map is a bestiary name.
