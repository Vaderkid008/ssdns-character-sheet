    Gives the Full kit for their calling (armor, pack, kit gear, background gear and pouch ES). Anything they already have isn't given twice.

    Act I loot tables are v1.4. Locked: each of the five guns is 20%, and an Outlaw Stash turns up Black shards on a 1 (1-in-6). Glowing Letters are a scroll-chance after the picks (Saddlebag 70%, Supply Crate 65%, Outlaw Stash 85% then 35%, Strongbox 95% then 50%). Letter mix is locked Friendlier: sealed 35%, Cure 24%, Shield 24%, Fireball 12%, Revivify 5%. These percentages are the balance floor. The chapter meter warns only. A hand-pick still lands. Select the chest for Lockpick? Yes / No, Force, or Use key.

    Big game is yours to set: a deer is about 6 meals (item "Deer", meals 6). Bread, cheese and meat chunks are ½ meal; liquor is 0 meals and 0 gal.
